COMPETITION GOALS

NOVA was designed to support sustained lunar operations through these mission goals:

  1. Repair existing lunar infrastructure, including solar panels, habitat modules, communication arrays, and other rovers.
  2. Connect umbilicals for fluid, data, and power transfers.
  3. Transport payloads up to 800 kg around the lunar base.
  4. Monitor the surrounding lunar environment using vision, optics, and sensors.
  5. Autonomously navigate the lunar surface, traversing slopes up to 20° and obstacles up to 0.2 m.
  6. Define NOVA’s process from Earth to Moon, including its development timeline, launch, and tasks.
